Lonestar Family Health Clinic Expansion

Enhancing Healthcare Accessibility Through Strategic Design and Project Management

Explore Project

About the Project

  • Type: Professional project with a small team

  • Role: Design Lead & Project Manager

  • Timeline: 3 years (2022 to 2024)

  • Tools Used: User Interviews, Healthcare Facility Planning, Spatial Design, Interior Design, 3D Visualization, Cross Functional Coordination

  • Objective: Lead all phases of the project from discovery through construction administration, owning stakeholder alignment, design decision making, and cross functional coordination across architects, engineers, contractors, and municipal agencies to deliver a state of the art behavioral health facility.


Overview

The Lone Star Family Health Clinic Expansion in Conroe, TX, is a $9.5M project aimed at transforming a two-story medical office building into a comprehensive healthcare facility. The expansion added a 26,000 sq. ft. Behavioral Health Clinic and enhanced administrative spaces, reflecting the growing needs of the community. My role as an Architectural Designer involved managing all phases, from pre-development to construction administration, ensuring the project’s successful execution.


Why This Project Matters for PM

Every square foot of a behavioral health clinic is a decision with human stakes. This project required the same core skills as digital product management: deeply understanding user needs before defining the solution, aligning diverse stakeholders around a shared vision, making scope and priority tradeoffs under real constraints, and driving execution across a large cross functional team over a multi year timeline.

Managing a $9.5M project from pre-development through a 24 month construction phase is a master class in stakeholder management, risk mitigation, and delivering outcomes against competing demands.


Users and Collaborators

  • Users: Healthcare providers, administrative staff, and patients relying on enhanced behavioral health services.

  • Design Collaborators: Architects, engineers, contractors, and municipal agencies collaborating to deliver a functional and compliant facility.

PM Skills Demonstrated

  • Stakeholder Management: Owned client relationships and facilitated alignment across 10+ disciplines including structural, mechanical, electrical, and civil engineers, contractors, and municipal agencies.

  • Scope and Priority Management: Conducted value engineering to optimize scope against a $9.5M budget without compromising the core user experience for patients and staff.

  • Cross Functional Coordination: Led bi weekly OAC meetings, managed change requests, and resolved interdisciplinary conflicts across a 24 month execution timeline.

  • Risk Mitigation: Identified and resolved design conflicts early in documentation phase to prevent costly field changes during construction.

  • Client Communication: Presented design decisions using 3D renderings, animated flythroughs, and material samples to make complex tradeoffs legible to non technical stakeholders.


Project Process and Timeline

1. Discovery: Client Interviews and Constraints Mapping (6 months)

  • Conducted initial client meetings as structured user interviews, understanding clinical workflows, patient populations, and operational goals before any spatial design began.

  • Established contracts, consultant scopes, and timelines to align all parties on deliverables and profitability.

  • Researched jurisdictional regulations to define compliance constraints early.

2. Define and Ideate: Spatial Layouts and Design Exploration (4 months)

  • Translated client and user needs into spatial layouts through site surveys and iterative design exploration.

  • Led client review sessions using 3D renderings, animated flythroughs, and physical material samples to make abstract design decisions tangible and drive alignment.

  • Collaborated with consultants to finalize spatial designs and specify materials.

3. Specify: Translating Design Intent into Technical Documentation (2 months)

  • Produced detailed architectural drawings translating design decisions into precise technical specifications across structural, mechanical, and electrical requirements.

  • Coordinated with consultants to resolve conflicts such as sewage systems, structural supports, and fire exits before they reached the field.

  • Reviewed QA/QC feedback to ensure all consultant drawings preserved design intent.

4. Implementation: Stakeholder Management Through Build (24 months)

  • Led public bidding for a General Contractor and coordinated permit applications across all consultants.

  • Drove value engineering decisions to reduce construction costs while maintaining quality and user experience standards.

  • Managed shop drawings, owner change requests, and bi weekly OAC meetings to track progress and resolve challenges.

  • Performed monthly site visits to verify timeline adherence and quality standards.

Project Takeaways

Managing a large scale facility project over 3 years taught me that the core skills of product management translate directly across physical and digital contexts: understand your users before defining your solution, align stakeholders around shared goals, make principled tradeoffs under real constraints, and drive execution with accountability.

The skills I carried most directly into product work: mapping user journeys before drawing any walls, communicating design rationale to non technical stakeholders through visual artifacts, and navigating competing priorities across a large cross functional team with no single source of authority.
